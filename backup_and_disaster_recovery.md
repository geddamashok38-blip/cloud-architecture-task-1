# Backup and Disaster Recovery

## Objectives
Protect business data against accidental deletion, corruption, infrastructure failure, and operational incidents.

## Strategy
- Automated database backups
- Point-in-time recovery where supported
- Object storage versioning
- Separate backup storage/location
- Defined retention policy
- Regular restore testing

## Assumed Targets
- RPO: 15 minutes
- RTO: 1 hour

## Recovery
1. Detect and classify incident.
2. Protect remaining data.
3. Select recovery point.
4. Restore data.
5. Validate integrity.
6. Restore application service.
7. Monitor and document the incident.

These targets are hypothetical project assumptions, not customer requirements.
